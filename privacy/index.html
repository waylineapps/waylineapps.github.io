Tarmac & Trail Privacy Policy

Tarmac & Trail: Ride Journal · last updated 9 November 2026

The short version

Tarmac & Trail never sells or shares your data. There are no adverts, no trackers and no data brokers. Your trips stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

Stored only on your phone

Your recorded trips, routes, photos, notes, Moments and settings are kept on your phone. Tarmac & Trail does not upload them. Deleting the app deletes them. You can export any trip as a GPX file to back it up.

Location

Tarmac & Trail uses your precise location to record trips. While a recording is running it also uses location in the background, so the trip keeps recording with the screen off. When you are not recording, Tarmac & Trail does not track you.

Photos and camera

If you add a photo to a Moment, Tarmac & Trail uses the camera or your photo library to get it and keeps a copy on your phone. If you save a story card, Tarmac & Trail saves the image to your photos. Photos are not uploaded.

Motion sensors

During a recording, Tarmac & Trail uses the phone's motion sensors to estimate lean angle and braking, and to spot a possible crash. This stays on your phone.

Imported routes

If a GPX file you import has no heights, Tarmac & Trail looks them up once from Open-Meteo (a free elevation service) by sending just the route's map points. Nothing about you is sent, and nothing is kept by us.

Online features (only if you use them)

Tarmac & Trail signs you in anonymously. It doesn't ask for a name, email or password.

  • Ride with a mate: the name you type and your live position are shared only with riders who have your ride code. They are removed when you leave the ride.
  • Road ratings: your scores and comments for a road are stored so other riders can see the road's overall score and your tips (shown without your name). Riders can report a comment or block a rider; reports are kept so we can review them, and a comment reported by several riders is hidden.
  • Crash alerts: if a crash alert is sent, the message and your location go to the phone numbers you chose, through our text message provider (Twilio). The numbers are kept on your phone. They pass through our server only to send the text and are not stored there. We keep a record of when alerts were sent, to stop misuse.
  • Tarmac & Trail Pro: subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the App Store, and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether you have Pro. RevenueCat sees an anonymous ID and your purchase history, not your name or card details.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: our online server, for the online features above.
  • Twilio: crash alert texts only.
  • RevenueCat: Tarmac & Trail Pro subscription status.
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap and MapTiler: map and satellite images. They see which map area is being loaded, not who you are.

We only send each of them what its feature needs.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Your data → Delete my online data. This removes your road ratings, ride-with-a-mate details, live positions and crash alert records from our server straight away. Deleting a trip removes it from your phone. You can also email us and we will delete your online data for you.

Children

Tarmac & Trail is not intended for children under 13.
